How to play

  • Move: ← → / A D. On touch screens, hold the left or right half of the screen.
  • Jump: ↑ / W / Space (hold longer to jump higher). On touch screens, swipe up.
  • Collect every ring to open the exit portal.
  • Avoid spikes and red spiky hazards.
  • Springs launch you high into the air.
  • Pink blocks are bouncy. Hold jump as you land for a super bounce.
  • Checkpoints save your respawn point.
  • Pause: Esc / P   Restart: R   Gamepads work too.
