How to play

  • Move: ← → / A D. On touch screens, use the ◀ ▶ buttons.
  • Jump: ↑ / W / Space (hold longer to jump higher). On touch screens, use the JUMP button.
  • Collect every ring to unlock the exit door.
  • Avoid spikes and the red spiky spiders.
  • Springs launch you high into the air.
  • Pink blocks are bouncy. Hold jump as you land for a super bounce.
  • Checkpoints save your respawn point.
  • Pumps blow you up big: you jump higher and float in water, but can't fit through 1-tile gaps.
  • Pins shrink you: you sink in water and fit under low slabs.
  • Water makes you float. Blowers push you up into the air.
  • Every level hides a secret lever behind fake bricks. Find it to play a bonus pinball table: ← → flip the flippers, and you leave with the points.
  • Pause: Esc / P   Restart: R   Gamepads work too.
